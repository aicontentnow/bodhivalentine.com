01

AI Film Studio: proposed, proven, adopted

Harmonic Inc. (NASDAQ: HLIT)

Proposed the AI video production operating model to leadership, was assigned to lead it, and proved it across two proofs of concept. The first was a complete 35-shot explainer directed end to end against a ten working day deadline, with a team spread across France and Denver: a graphic designer, a videographer and editor, and a video production graduate. None of them had used any of the tools, two were French native speakers, and all three arrived with very different appetites for change. The second I built independently and took to leadership as a business case. Both were approved, and the processes and tools now run all Harmonic video storytelling, external and internal.

The production pipeline and prompt optimizer

Custom tooling · Built, not bought

A 46-node pipeline spanning seven-plus AI platforms, taking script to storyboard to high-resolution first and last frames to finished clips with audio. Alongside it, a prompt optimizer that reads the generated storyboard back against the original script treatment and writes the timestamped direction between keyframes. Clip generation went from hours per scene to fifteen minutes.
